Privacy Policy for BuzzStormNow

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: Immediate

At BuzzStormNow, accessible from https://buzzstormnow.blogspot.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by BuzzStormNow and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, do not hesitate to contact our editorial privacy team at contact@buzzstormnow.com or legal@buzzstormnow.com.

1. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Content Policy and agree to their terms.

2. Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide your personal information.

  • Direct Inquiries: If you contact us directly via email (e.g. for news tips, copyright notices, or editorial queries), we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, the contents of the message, attachments, and any other information you choose to provide.
  • Newsletter & Subscriptions: When you subscribe to our newsletters or participate in reader polls and discussions, we may ask for your contact information, such as your email address.
  • Comments: When visitors leave comments on the site, standard comment profile details and IP addresses are recorded to help with spam detection.

3. Log Files & Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

BuzzStormNow follows standard industry procedures of using server log files. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement across the website, and gathering broad demographic information.

We utilize Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-JPJGK7GW5Q) to track aggregated reader interactions, reading durations, and site performance to deliver a faster, high-quality user experience.

4. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Google is a third-party advertising vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, known as DoubleClick DART cookies and advertising identifiers, to serve relevant ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to buzzstormnow.blogspot.com and other sites across the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or the Your Online Choices Portal (for European users).

BuzzStormNow's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. 6. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), among other rights, California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of personal data, and demand that the business not sell their personal data. BuzzStormNow does not sell personal information to data brokers or third parties.

7. GDPR Data Protection Rights & EU Digital Services Act (DSA)

We are fully committed to protecting European Union citizens' rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the EU Digital Services Act (DSA):

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Right to Object & Restrict Processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.
  • DSA Dispute Settlement: Under the EU DSA, you also have the right to appeal content actions and utilize certified dispute settlement bodies.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting children online is an absolute priority. BuzzStormNow strictly complies with the Google Blogger Policy on Child Safety and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect or solicit any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it from our records.
