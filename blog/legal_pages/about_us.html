🌟 Editorial Standards & Fact-Checking Methodology

BuzzStormNow upholds rigorous standards aligned with Google's E-E-A-T framework and the Blogger Content Policy:

  • Primary Source Verification: All corporate disclosures, market movements, and regulatory filings are cross-checked directly against primary records (such as NSE/BSE archives, SEBI press notes, company regulatory filings, and government publications).
  • Human Editorial Review: While we leverage AI tools to assist in parsing vast data archives and drafting summaries, all content is directed, edited, and approved by human editorial staff.
  • Anti-Clickbait Commitment: We reject misleading headlines, speculative rumors, and sensationalized claims. Our headlines strictly reflect verifiable facts.
  • Editorial Independence: Our coverage is completely independent of external corporate sponsors, advertisers, or political interest groups.
