Content Policy & Editorial Guidelines

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective immediately in full alignment with Google Blogger Content Policies

Welcome to BuzzStormNow (https://buzzstormnow.blogspot.com). As an independent digital publication curating viral news, technology breakthroughs, financial markets, and global developments, we hold ourselves to the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and safety.

Core Principle: BuzzStormNow operates in strict compliance with the Google Blogger Content Policy, Google AdSense Program Policies, and Google Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines (E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness). This document defines our editorial principles, prohibited content standards, AI-assisted research ethics, and reader reporting mechanisms.

1. AI-Assisted Journalism & Content Integrity Standard

BuzzStormNow leverages modern artificial intelligence technologies exclusively as assistive tools for data parsing, structured summarization, and trend detection. We adhere to clear principles regarding automated content:

  • Human-in-the-Loop Oversight: Every dispatch published under the BuzzStormNow banner undergoes human editorial validation, fact-checking, and contextual review prior to publication.
  • Zero Tolerance for Synthetic Spam: In direct compliance with Blogger's prohibition on "unwanted content that is created by an automated program, unwanted repetitive content, nonsensical content, or anything that appears to be a mass solicitation," we do not publish mass-scraped, unverified, or auto-spun filler articles.
  • High Informational Value: Every article includes original editorial context, comparative data tables, structured analysis, and direct source attribution.

2. Prohibition of Misleading Content & Manipulated Media

In accordance with Blogger's guidelines against deceptive content:

  • Truthful Headlines: We reject deceptive clickbait, false promises, and sensationalized misrepresentations. Headlines must accurately reflect the underlying facts reported in primary disclosures.
  • Civic and Democratic Processes: We strictly prohibit demonstrably false claims concerning voting procedures, candidate eligibility, census participation, or false reports regarding the health or status of public officials.
  • Manipulated & Doctored Media: We never create, publish, or distribute deepfakes, technically manipulated video, or doctored imagery intended to deceive readers or cause harm.
  • Health & Medical Integrity: We do not publish misleading medical claims, unverified health remedies, or content that contradicts established scientific consensus and public health guidance.
  • Conspiracy Theories: We strictly prohibit baseless conspiracy theories that promote harm, violence, or discrimination against any group or individual.

3. Financial & Market Information Standard (SEBI & Global Compliance)

Educational Notice: BuzzStormNow is not a registered investment advisor, broker, or portfolio manager under SEBI (India), the US SEC, or any other financial regulatory agency.
  • All articles covering equities, IPOs, corporate actions, commodities, and macroeconomic indicators are published strictly for educational and informational purposes.
  • We never publish "get rich quick" schemes, guaranteed return claims, pump-and-dump promotion, or personalized investment recommendations.
  • Financial metrics, earnings data, and board outcomes are verified against official exchange archives (e.g., National Stock Exchange of India, Bombay Stock Exchange, SEBI, and official company regulatory filings) with source links provided.
  • Readers are explicitly advised to conduct independent due diligence and consult certified financial planners before executing market transactions.

4. Prohibited Content Categories

BuzzStormNow enforces a zero-tolerance policy against any material prohibited by the Google Blogger Content Policy:

  • Adult and Explicit Content: BuzzStormNow is a family-safe, general-audience publication. We strictly forbid sexually explicit imagery, non-consensual explicit imagery (NCEI), or commercial adult services.
  • Child Safety & Exploitation: We strictly prohibit any content that exploits, abuses, or endangers children. Any suspected violations will be immediately removed and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate law enforcement authorities.
  • Hate Speech & Harassment: We do not tolerate content that promotes violence, incites hatred, bullies, or marginalizes individuals based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, nationality, sexual orientation, gender, or veteran status.
  • Malware, Phishing & Dangerous Code: BuzzStormNow maintains a clean, secure technical infrastructure. We do not host, link to, or transmit malware, spyware, unauthorized downloads, or deceptive phishing portals.
  • Regulated Goods & Illegal Activities: We prohibit facilitating the sale of illicit pharmaceuticals, weapons, fireworks, gambling services, tobacco, alcohol, or unauthorized restricted services.
  • Personal and Confidential Information: We do not publish private contact details, government IDs, banking data, or confidential medical records without authorized legal consent.

5. Copyright, Fair Use & DMCA Takedown Policy

BuzzStormNow respects the intellectual property rights of creators, journalists, and corporate entities:

  • Attribution: Secondary reporting, press quotes, and data points are accompanied by clear attribution and links to the primary publisher or regulatory filing.
  • Fair Use: Brief excerpts and public press releases are utilized under statutory Fair Use principles (news reporting, commentary, and public criticism).
  • DMCA / Copyright Notices: If you are a copyright owner or legal representative and believe your work has been utilized in a manner that constitutes infringement, please submit a formal notice to our designated Copyright Desk at:
    Email: legal@buzzstormnow.com (Subject: DMCA Takedown Notice)
  • Resolution SLA: Our legal desk reviews and acts upon valid infringement notifications within 24 to 48 business hours, including immediate unpublishing or redaction of contested materials.

6. Transparency, Identity & Anti-Impersonation

BuzzStormNow is an independent media initiative. We do not impersonate government agencies, regulatory bodies (such as NSE, BSE, RBI, SEC), or other media organizations. Our authors and research desk operate transparently, with verified contact channels provided across all pages.

7. Content Moderation, DSA & Reader Dispute Redress

We actively monitor our publication and invite our global audience to assist in maintaining community standards:

  • Native Blogger Reporting: Readers can flag any post directly using Blogger's built-in reporting tool via the Blogger Abuse Reporting Form.
  • Direct Editorial Inquiries: To request a factual correction or file a grievance, email contact@buzzstormnow.com. We evaluate correction requests within 48 hours.
  • EU Digital Services Act (DSA) Compliance: European Union users possess the right to seek out-of-court dispute settlement through certified dispute bodies if they contest content actions, as detailed under the European Union's DSA framework.
