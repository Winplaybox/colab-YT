# 🎨 EpicBharatSaga — Deity Iconography & Character Bible
> **Standard Iconographic Blueprint to Guarantee Visual Consistency Across All Museum & Cinematic Visuals**

---

## 🔱 Why an Iconographic Bible is Mandatory
In Hindu iconography, every deity's skin tone (*varna*), hand posture (*mudra*), weapon (*ayudha*), ornament (*bhushana*), vehicle (*vahana*), and environment carries profound cosmic and philosophical meaning.

Uncurated AI generation frequently commits severe iconographical blunders:
- Giving deities random numbers of arms (e.g. 5 arms or 3 arms)
- Placing the wrong weapons in hands (e.g. Vishnu holding a trident)
- Wrong vehicles (e.g. Shiva riding a lion)
- Inaccurate skin tones (e.g. cartoonish neon colors instead of traditional *shyama-varna* or *gauranga*)

Every image selected or generated for **EpicBharatSaga** must strictly conform to the specifications below.

## 1. 🦚 Lord Vishnu (Narayana / Madhava)
*The Preserver of the Cosmos*

- **Complexion (*Varna*)**: *Megha-Shyama* (deep raincloud blue / dark slate blue with radiant luminescent aura). Never neon blue.
- **Form & Arms**: *Chaturbhuja* (4 arms):
  - **Upper Right**: *Sudarshana Chakra* (blazing 108-serrated solar discus radiating flames).
  - **Upper Left**: *Panchajanya Shankha* (pure white cosmic conch shell representing primordial sound *Nada-Brahma*).
  - **Lower Left**: *Kaumodaki Gada* (golden cosmic mace resting vertically or in hand).
  - **Lower Right**: *Padma* (divine blooming pink lotus flower / *Varada mudra*).
- **Garments & Ornaments**:
  - *Pitambara*: Luminous golden-yellow royal silk dhoti.
  - *Kaustubha Mani*: The dazzling wish-fulfilling ruby/gem glowing on his chest.
  - *Shrivatsa*: Sacred auspicious white mark on the chest representing Goddess Lakshmi.
  - *Vanamala*: Long fragrant garland of celestial forest flowers reaching down to his knees.
  - *Kirita-Mukuta*: Regal, tall, jeweled golden crown.
- **Vehicle (*Vahana*)**: *Garuda* (celestial king of birds with golden feathers and eagle-human form).
- **Cosmic Bed**: *Adishesha / Ananta Shesha* (thousand-headed serpent coiled upon the cosmic ocean of milk, *Ksheerasagara*).
- **Cosmic Abode**: *Vaikuntha* (transcendental golden realm of eternal bliss).
- **Forbidden Errors**: ❌ Never depict Vishnu with a Trishula (Trident). ❌ Never depict without yellow garments (*Pitambara*). ❌ Never depict with fewer than 4 arms in his cosmic form.

## 2. 🔱 Lord Shiva (Mahadeva / Shankara)
*The Auspicious Lord of Ascetics & Cosmic Dissolution*

- **Complexion (*Varna*)**: *Karpura-Gauranga* (radiant camphor-white / ash-smeared *Bhasma* over fair-golden skin).
- **Neck (*Kanth*)**: *Neelkanth* (radiant lapis-lazuli blue throat from the Halahala cosmic poison).
- **Form & Arms**: Usually *Dvibhuja* (2 arms in ascetic form) or *Chaturbhuja* (4 arms in divine manifestation).
  - **Ayudhas**: *Trishula* (three-pronged spear representing Sattva, Rajas, Tamas and the three gunas/times), *Damru* (hourglass drum emitting the sacred 14 Maheshwara sutras).
- **Distinctive Attributes**:
  - *Trinetra*: Vertical third eye on forehead radiating cosmic fire.
  - *Chandrama*: Crescent moon tucked into his matted dreadlocks (*Jata*).
  - *Ganga*: Celestial river Ganga spouting in crystalline torrents from his crown of hair.
  - *Vasuki*: Great serpent coiled three times around his neck like a sacred necklace.
  - *Rudraksha*: Sacred beads adorning his wrists, arms, neck, and ears.
  - *Vyaghra-Charma*: Tiger skin garment wrapped around the waist.
- **Vehicle (*Vahana*)**: *Nandi* (majestic white bull with humped back, golden horns, and brass bell collar).
- **Cosmic Abode**: *Mount Kailash* (pristine Himalayan snow-covered peak surrounded by Lake Manasarovar).
- **Forbidden Errors**: ❌ Never depict Shiva with a royal crown or gold armor (he is the supreme ascetic *Yogi*). ❌ Never depict without the crescent moon and Trishula. ❌ Never depict Nandi as anything other than a majestic white bull.

## 3. 🦁 Goddess Durga (Mahashakti / Chandika)
*The Supreme Invincible Mother of the Cosmos*

- **Complexion (*Varna*)**: Radiant glowing molten gold (*Tapta-Kanchana-Sannibha*).
- **Form & Arms**: *Ashtabhuja* (8 arms) or *Dashabhuja* (10 arms), each wielding divine weapons bestowed by the devas:
  - Trishula (gift of Shiva), Sudarshana Chakra (Vishnu), Vajra & Bell (Indra), Gada & Pasha (Yama & Varuna), Bow & Arrows (Vayu), Sword & Shield, Lotus (Brahma).
- **Garments & Ornaments**:
  - Brilliant royal crimson-red silk saree with heavy gold zari borders.
  - Elaborate golden crown (*Kireeta*), nose ring (*Nath*), radiant third eye, and red *Alta* on hands/feet.
- **Vehicle (*Vahana*)**: *Simha* (golden royal lion) or *Vyaghra* (fierce tiger).
- **Posture**: *Mahishasuramardini* (heroic *Pratyalidha* stance, impaling the demon Mahishasura emerging from the buffalo carcass).
- **Forbidden Errors**: ❌ Never depict Durga without weapons or looking frightened. ❌ Never depict her vehicle as a domestic animal. ❌ Face must radiate calm, divine, serene majesty even in the midst of cosmic battle.

## 4. 🐒 Lord Hanuman (Maruti / Bajrangbali)
*The Embodiment of Supreme Devotion, Strength, and Humility*

- **Complexion (*Varna*)**: *Sindoor-Varna* (golden-orange radiant skin smeared with sacred vermilion).
- **Body (*Deha*)**: *Vajra-Deha* (colossal, muscular, adamantine build radiating golden light).
- **Attributes**:
  - Golden *Gada* (mace) held in the left hand or resting on shoulder.
  - In Bhakti posture: Hands folded in *Anjali Mudra* or tearing open chest to reveal Sita-Rama enshrined in his heart.
  - In Vir-Rasa posture: Soaring through the sky carrying the glowing golden mountain *Dronagiri* (with Sanjeevani herbs) on the palm of his hand.
- **Garments**: Saffron or yellow kaupina/langot with sacred Yajnopavita (sacred thread) across the torso.
- **Forbidden Errors**: ❌ Never depict Hanuman looking arrogant or aggressive towards innocent beings. ❌ Always maintain the monkey-divine facial anatomy with noble, serene eyes.

## 5. 🐘 Lord Ganesha (Vighnaharta / Ganapati)
*The Remover of Obstacles & Master of Intellect*

- **Complexion (*Varna*)**: Golden-red / Haldi yellow / Sindoor crimson.
- **Head & Tusks**: Elephant head with noble curved trunk (*Vakratunda*), single intact left tusk and broken right tusk (*Ekadanta*).
- **Form & Arms**: *Chaturbhuja* (4 arms):
  - **Upper Right**: *Ankusha* (elephant goad / axe to sever attachments).
  - **Upper Left**: *Pasha* (divine noose to capture all difficulties).
  - **Lower Left**: Golden bowl of *Modaks* / sweet laddus (spiritual sweetness of liberation).
  - **Lower Right**: *Abhaya Mudra* (gesture of fearlessness and protection).
- **Garments**: Yellow or red royal silk with snake tied as a belly belt (*Udarabandha*).
- **Vehicle (*Vahana*)**: *Mushaka* (clever mouse / rat sitting faithfully at his feet looking up).
- **Forbidden Errors**: ❌ Never depict both tusks broken or both fully intact. ❌ Never depict without the Modaka bowl.

## 6. 🪈 Lord Krishna (Gopala / Parthasarathi)
*The Supreme Enchanter & Preceptor of the Gita*

- **Complexion (*Varna*)**: *Megha-Shyama* (mesmerizing twilight dark blue-black like a monsoon storm cloud).
- **Attributes**:
  - *Mor-Pankh*: A vibrant peacock feather tucked into the side of his hair crest.
  - *Bansuri (Venu)*: Golden bamboo flute held gracefully at his lips.
  - *Pitambara*: Yellow silk dhoti draped with effortless royal elegance.
  - *Vanamala*: Wildflower garland.
  - In *Kurukshetra* form: Charioteer (*Parthasarathi*) holding the reins of 4 white divine horses, Sudarshana Chakra emerging during Vishwaroop.
- **Forbidden Errors**: ❌ Never depict Krishna looking aged, frail, or grim. His face must always carry the signature subtle transcendental smile (*Mridu-Hasa*).

## 7. 🏹 Lord Rama (Kodanda-Rama / Maryada Purushottama)
*The Ideal King & Embodiment of Righteousness*

- **Complexion (*Varna*)**: *Shyama-Sundara* (gentle noble dark slate blue or fair-wheatish royal complexion).
- **Attributes**:
  - *Kodanda*: Mighty divine bow slung over his broad shoulder.
  - Quiver of inexhaustible golden arrows (*Akshaya Tunira*) strapped to his back.
  - Gentle, dignified, poised stance of unshakeable moral rectitude.
- **Attire**:
  - *Vanavasa period*: Ascetic bark garments (*Valkala*), matted hair tied simply, unadorned yet radiating natural regal majesty.
  - *Ayodhya Pattabhisheka*: Royal crown, white silk, jewels, seated beside Queen Sita on the golden throne.
- **Forbidden Errors**: ❌ Never depict Rama without his bow and arrows. ❌ Never depict him in aggressive rage; even in battle his posture is majestic, calm, and purposeful.
